🚀 Getting Started

How to use Conjure

Step 1 → Click "New Character" in the sidebar to create a character. Fill in their physical appearance, clothing, and other details. Save them with a name.


Step 2 → Go to the Scene Builder tab. Select which characters appear in the scene using the chips at the top.


Step 3 → Describe each character's specific action in the scene, the environment, lighting, mood, and composition.


Step 4 → Configure your Generation Settings — image style (realistic vs cartoon), aspect ratio, resolution, and quality boosters.


Step 5 → Click "Generate Prompt". Copy the result for Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, DALL·E, or any AI image platform.


NSFW → Toggle NSFW in the header. When OFF, mature content descriptors are automatically replaced with safe alternatives. When ON, full uncensored prompts are included.

💡 Tips for Consistent Characters

• Be very specific with physical features (exact eye color, hair length/style, facial features, distinguishing marks)


• Include a reference to the person's unique identifiers like scars, tattoos, freckles, or a specific jawline shape


• For consistent body type use seed values in your AI tool alongside this prompt


• In Midjourney: use --cref with a reference image for best consistency


• In Stable Diffusion: use LoRA files trained on a specific character for true consistency
